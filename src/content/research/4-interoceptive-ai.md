This was the product of incredible teamwork. To develop this theoretical account, covering fields from cybernetics, computational biology, and machine learning to active inference, we held a weekly discussion club from 2021. During this, we invited Karl Friston (a co-author of this paper) to [share his ideas with us](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qxqhmlm5LvQ), and he gave invaluable input throughout. The core idea of this perspective is that, for autonomous and adaptive agents, we should build the principle of survival, in a mathematically abstracted form, into artificial agents. The benchmark paper for this perspective is out at NeurIPS 2025 (Datasets and Benchmarks track). Click below for the full text, if you want to gain more insight.
