The signal-flow hierarchy that I recovered from fMRI aligned significantly with the cortical hierarchy proposed by M. Mesulam in his textbook *Principles of Behavioral and Cognitive Neurology*, which was used in the seminal paper by [Margulies et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1608282113) to define the *“functional hierarchy.”* However, that was an implicit hierarchy with no defined direction, and it was ambiguous about the role of the paralimbic cortex. Furthermore, my signal-flow hierarchy showed the quintessential nature of flexibility in the brain by demonstrating state-dependent changes. Importantly, the overall structure of the hierarchy (the ordering from the most external to the most internal) did not change, indicating the universal role of internally driven top-down modulation of external signals.
